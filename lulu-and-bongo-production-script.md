# LULU & BONGO
## Children's Animated Compilation — Full Production Script
### Runtime: 22–26 minutes | Format: 5–6 Episodes × 3–5 minutes each

---

## SERIES OVERVIEW

**Title:** Lulu & Bongo  
**Target Audience:** Ages 2–6  
**Format:** Episodic slapstick comedy, no dialogue  
**Visual Style:** Bright 2D flat-color animation, thick black outlines, bouncy exaggerated physics  
**Audio:** Orchestral/ukulele underscore + cartoon SFX only. No voice acting.

---

## CHARACTER BIBLE

### LULU
- **Age:** ~5 years old
- **Design:** Small, round body. Big circular eyes (dark brown irises, white highlight dot). Rosy circular cheeks. Short dark hair with a **red polka-dot headband**. Wears a **yellow-and-white horizontal striped dress** and tiny red shoes.
- **Personality:** Bold, fearless, perpetually excited. She never reads the room. Chaos follows her not out of malice — she simply sees the world as a giant playground.
- **Movement:** Fast, springy, arms-flapping-run. Bounces in place when excited. Trips on nothing. Lands on her feet every time.
- **Expressions used frequently:** Wide sparkle eyes (wonder), squinty grin (about to do something), hands on cheeks (oops!), arms wide (ta-da!).

### BONGO
- **Age:** Adult bear, wise but easily flustered
- **Design:** Large, round, warm brown bear. Small round ears. Soft belly. Wears a **green apron** (when cooking), **blue overalls** (outdoor scenes), or **striped nightcap** (sleeping scenes). Big expressive eyebrows — his entire emotional range lives there.
- **Personality:** Gentle, patient, loves order and routine. Not grumpy — just perpetually surprised by Lulu. He always forgives her and secretly loves the chaos.
- **Movement:** Slow and deliberate normally. Frantic and stumbling when flustered. Wide-open eyes = panic. Half-closed eyes = exhausted disbelief.
- **Expressions used frequently:** Eyebrows shot up (shock), slow blink (disbelief), head in paws (despair), wide toothy grin (genuine joy).

# EPISODE 1: "THE GREAT SOUP DISASTER" 🍲
**Runtime: ~4 minutes | Lesson: Helping a Friend**

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Bright, staccato ukulele jingle — 4 bars]**

- Title card bounces in: **"Episode 1: The Great Soup Disaster 🍲"**
- Small cartoon pot icon steams beside the text.
- Jingle ends with a cymbal sting.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (40 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Calm, warm kitchen melody. Soft woodwinds.]**

**SCENE: Bongo's cabin kitchen. Morning light streams through a round window. Pots hang on the wall. A wooden table holds vegetables.**

- Bongo hums silently (exaggerated mouth movements), tying his green apron.
- He places a **large blue pot** on the stove. Drops in carrots, then potatoes, then a big bunch of herbs — each one with a satisfying *plop* SFX.
- He stirs slowly, looking proud. Steam rises in cute curlicues.
- He tastes a spoonful. His eyebrows raise in delight. He does a tiny happy shoulder shimmy.
- He turns to set the table — two bowls, two spoons, a checkered cloth. He clearly made enough for two.

**[VISUAL LESSON SEED: Bongo made extra soup to share with someone.]**

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (25 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Sudden upbeat pop — faster tempo, higher pitch]**

**[SFX: BOING BOING BOING — running footsteps getting louder]**

- The cabin door BLASTS open. Lulu stands in the doorway, backlit dramatically (comedic effect).
- She SNIFFS the air. Her eyes go wide-wide-WIDE. Sparkle stars appear in her pupils.
- She points at the pot. Grins. Makes a running start—

**[SFX: WHOOOOSH]**

- —and slides across the kitchen floor in her socks, CRASHING into Bongo's legs.
- Bongo grabs the counter. Pot wobbles. Soup sloshes. One carrot flies out and lands on Bongo's head.
- Bongo looks at the carrot on his head. Looks at Lulu. Slow blink.
- Lulu: (no words — just a huge grin and two thumbs up).

---

## SEGMENT 4 — ESCALATION (110 seconds / ~1 min 50 sec)

**[MUSIC: Playful, slightly frantic. Brass stings on each beat of chaos.]**

### Round 1 — THE SPOON INCIDENT
- Lulu grabs the stirring spoon to "help."
- She stirs FAST. Too fast. Soup starts spinning like a whirlpool.
- Bongo waves his arms: *No no no!*
- **[SFX: WHOOOOOOOP — rising whine]**
- The spoon flings a potato chunk. It arcs in slow motion.
- Bongo watches it. Tracks it with one eye.
- It hits the window with a SPLAT.
- Both stare at it. Lulu giggles. Bongo's eyebrow twitches.

### Round 2 — THE HERB AVALANCHE
- Lulu spots the shelf of herb jars. Grabs one. Sniffs it. Makes a face (too spicy!).
- She grabs another. And another. Starts pouring them ALL in.
- Bongo's eyes go huge. He lunges for the jars—
- **[SFX: POP POP POP POP]** — jars tumble. A cloud of spices puffs out.
- Both disappear in a colored smoke cloud.
- The cloud clears: Bongo is covered in purple and yellow spice dust. He looks like a polka-dot bear.
- Lulu looks at him, claps once, delighted. His eyebrow twitches again.

### Round 3 — THE LID LAUNCH
- Bongo recovers, puts the lid firmly on the pot. Crosses his arms. *There. Done.*
- Lulu stares at the pot. Tilts her head. Sneaks toward it.
- She presses her ear against it — hearing the bubbling — eyes wide with curiosity.
- She reaches up and touches the lid.
- **[SFX: BBBBLLLLLLOP — pressure building]**
- Bongo sees this. His eyes go HUGE. He runs in slow motion.
- The lid ROCKETS off the pot like a tiny flying saucer.
- **[SFX: WHEEEEEEE — lid spinning, GONG when it hits the ceiling]**
- Soup bubbles dramatically but stays in the pot (cute, not scary).
- The lid descends. Lands perfectly on Bongo's head like a hat.
- Beat. Beat. Beat.
- Lulu points and doubles over laughing (silent laugh, huge open mouth).

---

## SEGMENT 5 — CLIMAX (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Dramatic swell — full orchestra, one big comedic buildup note]**

- Bongo, pot-lid hat still on, reaches to take the pot off the stove.
- He grabs it — it's heavier than expected.
- He wobbles left. Wobbles right.
- Lulu tries to help — grabs the other handle — but she's tiny.
- **[SFX: SLIPPING SOUNDS — CARTOON SKID]**
- They spin once together, the pot between them — like a waltz — then—
- **[SFX: GIANT SPLOOOOOSH]**
- The soup erupts upward in a beautiful orange arc. Time slows.
- Every vegetable floats in the air. Bongo and Lulu stare up at them.
- Everything comes down.
- **[SFX: PLOP PLOP PLOP PLOP]**
- They are both covered head to toe in soup. Carrots on heads. A potato on Bongo's nose.
- Bongo's face: complete, total, silent defeat. He slumps forward.

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Gentle, warm, pizzicato strings. Soft and sweet.]**

- Lulu looks at Bongo. Looks at the mess. Looks at her hands.
- She waddles to the cabinet. Pulls out a can of **premade soup** (label has a bear face on it — "Bongo's Favorite").
- She holds it up to Bongo with the biggest, most hopeful grin.
- Bongo stares at it. Stares at her. His eyebrow slowly rises... then he bursts into a warm, silent laugh, belly shaking.
- He opens the can. Pours it into a small pot. Warms it quickly.
- They sit at the table together — both still soup-covered — and share the bowl.
- Lulu eats dramatically (big slurps mimed). Bongo shakes his head, smiling.
- **[VISUAL LESSON MOMENT: Lulu helped fix the problem she caused — helping a friend.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (10 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Short jingle — 3 bright ukulele chords + triangle ding]**

- Freeze frame: Lulu holds her soup spoon like a trophy. Bongo has a carrot still on his ear. Both are grinning.
- Text fades in: **"THE END 🍲"**
- Iris wipe to black.

# EPISODE 2: "BONGO'S GARDEN SURPRISE" 🌻
**Runtime: ~4 minutes | Lesson: Trying Again**

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Same jingle, slight variation — adds a flute trill]**

- Title card: **"Episode 2: Bongo's Garden Surprise 🌻"**
- A cartoon sunflower spins into frame beside the text.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (45 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Outdoorsy, cheerful. Acoustic guitar with birdsong SFX in background.]**

**SCENE: The sunny garden behind Bongo's cabin. Neat rows of soil. Seed packets lined up. A watering can. Gardening gloves.**

- Bongo marches out with a tiny shovel, wearing his blue overalls and a wide-brimmed sun hat.
- He consults a **hand-drawn planting chart** (very detailed, very organized — comedy in the precision).
- He digs a perfect row. Places seeds one by one with surgical precision.
- He waters exactly — counting silent drops.
- He steps back, wipes his brow, looks at his work with deep satisfaction.
- He puts up a small hand-painted sign: **"FUTURE GARDEN – DO NOT DISTURB"**

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (25 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Energetic tempo spike. Marimba melody.]**

**[SFX: RUMBLING — then Lulu ROLLS in on a wheelbarrow she's steering like a race car]**

- She hops off, spots the garden, spots the seeds, spots the watering can.
- Her eyes light up.
- She reads the "Do Not Disturb" sign. Tilts her head. Looks at it from the side.
- She clearly cannot read it. Smiles. Sets it down flat. Grabs the watering can.
- Bongo appears in the cabin doorway, sees this, and begins his slow-motion run: *Nooooo* (face only, no sound).

---

## SEGMENT 4 — ESCALATION (100 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Frenetic, tumbling melody. Snare drum on every stumble.]**

### Round 1 — THE FLOOD
- Lulu waters the garden. But she waters it A LOT. She refills the can. And again. And again.
- **[SFX: GLUGGLUGGLUGG... SPLOOOSH]**
- The garden rows turn into a muddy river. Seeds start floating.
- Bongo arrives. Stares at his floating seed packets sailing away like tiny boats.
- His sun hat slowly droops. His chart dissolves in the water.
- Lulu: doesn't notice. Still watering. Focused. Helpful!

### Round 2 — THE SEED MIX-UP
- Bongo rescues the seeds. Replants them carefully.
- Lulu finds the seed packets. Gets excited. Helps by pouring ALL of them in one spot.
- **[SFX: RATTLE RATTLE SPRINKLE]**
- Bongo looks at his planting chart. Looks at the chaotic seed pile. Looks at Lulu.
- He makes a valiant effort to sort them — holds up a seed, squints at it, compares it to the chart.
- They all look identical. He freezes.
- Lulu pats him on the arm reassuringly.

### Round 3 — THE FERTILIZER EXPLOSION
- Bongo finds a bag of **garden fertilizer** (cartoon bag with a big flower on it).
- He carefully measures a spoonful.
- Lulu grabs the bag. Wants to help. Pours the WHOLE bag.
- **[SFX: POOF — cloud of brown dust]**
- When the cloud settles, the seeds have sprouted INSTANTLY into massive, ridiculous cartoon plants — towering over both of them.
- **[SFX: SPRING BOIIIING × 12 as each plant shoots up]**
- One giant sunflower bends down and stares at Bongo at eye level.
- Bongo stares back at the sunflower. It blinks (cartoon eyes appear on it briefly).
- Bongo's hat falls off.

---

## SEGMENT 5 — CLIMAX (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Big comedic swell, tubas and horns]**

- The plants grow bigger. Bongo starts backing away.
- A giant pumpkin vine wraps around his ankle.
- **[SFX: CARTOON BOING]** — he's yanked off his feet.
- He hangs upside down from a vine, at eye level with Lulu.
- Upside-down Bongo. Normal Lulu. They look at each other.
- A cartoon bird lands on upside-down Bongo's belly, chirps once, flies away.
- Bongo sighs slowly (eyes half-close in exhausted acceptance).

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Warm, gentle. Soft flute.]**

- Lulu grabs Bongo's paws and pulls — tries to untangle him.
- The vine snaps. Both tumble backward into the mud.
- They sit up, mud-covered, surrounded by ridiculous cartoon giant vegetables.
- Lulu reaches behind a giant carrot. Pulls out a perfect, un-touched sunflower that somehow survived.
- She presents it to Bongo with both hands.
- Bongo takes it. Looks at it. His expression melts into a warm smile.
- He puts it in his overalls pocket like a boutonniere.
- They both look at the wild garden. Lulu points at the giant pumpkin enthusiastically.
- Bongo looks at it. Nods slowly. Maybe this garden is okay.
- **[VISUAL LESSON: They tried again together, and something good still grew.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (10 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Jingle. Bright and bouncy.]**

- Freeze frame: Bongo with a sunflower in his pocket. Lulu riding the giant pumpkin like a horse.
- Text: **"THE END 🌻"**

---

# EPISODE 3: "NAP TIME? WHAT NAP TIME?" 😴
**Runtime: ~3.5 minutes | Lesson: Being Quiet for Others**

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Softer, sleepy jingle version — same melody but slower, with a xylophone]**

- Title card: **"Episode 3: Nap Time? What Nap Time? 😴"**
- A cartoon "Zzzz" bubble drifts across the screen.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Soft lullaby underscore. Very quiet. Gentle harp.]**

**SCENE: Bongo's cabin living room. A giant armchair by the fireplace. Afternoon golden light.**

- Bongo yawns hugely (stretches arms wide, mouth open — silent but expressive).
- He fluffs a pillow. Arranges a blanket. Tests the chair. Perfect.
- He places a **"SLEEPING — PLEASE BE QUIET"** sign on the door.
- He sinks into the chair. Closes his eyes. A smile on his face.
- Within 3 seconds — cartoon "ZZZZ"s float up. He is asleep.

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (20 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Lulu's bouncy theme — but it cuts off immediately as she spots the sign]**

- Lulu bounds up to the door. Reads the sign. (She also can't read this one, but she sees Bongo's sleeping face drawn on it.)
- She puts a finger to her lips. Nods very seriously.
- Tiptoes in. Takes one huge exaggerated tiptoe step. Then another.
- Immediately trips over a rug.
- **[SFX: CRRASH — but then SILENCE]**
- Bongo stirs. One eye half-opens. Sees nothing. Goes back to sleep.
- Lulu: frozen mid-fall, one leg up. Grins. Tiptoes on.

---

## SEGMENT 4 — ESCALATION (90 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Starts near-silent. Each round gets incrementally louder underneath.]**

### Round 1 — THE CYMBAL THAT APPEARED FROM NOWHERE
- Lulu wanders the room, bored. Spots a music box. Winds it up softly.
- Lovely quiet music plays. She sways.
- The music box begins to speed up on its own.
- **[SFX: tinkle-tinkle-TINKLETINKLE-TINKTINKTINK—]**
- Then a tiny cartoon cymbal falls out of it.
- **[SFX: CRASSSHHH]**
- Bongo's eyes pop open. He looks around. Nothing. Eyes close.
- Lulu holds up the cymbal. Puts it down very carefully. It immediately falls again.
- **[SFX: clang]** (smaller). Bongo's ear twitches. Doesn't wake.

### Round 2 — THE SQUEAKY FLOORBOARD MAZE
- Lulu tries to cross the room. Every floorboard she steps on: SQUEAK.
- She discovers the squeaky board. Avoids it. Finds another squeaky one. Avoids it.
- She maps them in her head. Makes the most elaborate route around the room — climbing over furniture, ducking under the table, crawling.
- She almost makes it — steps on the ONE silent board — but accidentally knocks a stack of books.
- **[SFX: Books falling in slow motion: thwump... thwump... THWUMP THWUMP THWUMP THWUMP]**
- Bongo snorts. Turns over. Still asleep (miracle bear).

### Round 3 — THE MARCHING ANTS
- Lulu spots Bongo's collection of toy wind-up animals on the shelf.
- She wind them up carefully... one by one... they start walking.
- They all play tiny instruments (silent but illustrated — one has a drum, one a horn).
- They begin to march across the shelf. Off the shelf. Across the floor.
- One winds up marching ONTO Bongo's foot. Drum beating.
- Bongo's foot twitches. Then twitches again. Then—

---

## SEGMENT 5 — CLIMAX (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Total silence for 3 beats — then CRASH]**

- Lulu reaches for the drumming toy on Bongo's foot — leans over him —
- Her polka-dot headband slips off and falls—
- —lands directly on Bongo's nose—
- —with a tiny *boop* sound—
- Bongo's eyes OPEN. He sees Lulu six inches from his face, frozen, huge eyes.
- They stare.
- Lulu gives the world's tiniest, most apologetic wave.
- **[SFX: All the wind-up toys play a chaotic tiny fanfare in the background — tooting, drumming]**
- Bongo sits up slowly. The toys are all around him. The room is a soft chaos.
- His whole face collapses into exhausted disbelief.

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Sleepy, gentle, warm. Celesta melody.]**

- Bongo opens his mouth to react — but then yawns.
- Lulu's eyes go soft. She picks up her headband. Then picks up a small blanket from nearby.
- She drapes it over Bongo gently. Pats it down.
- She gathers ALL the wind-up toys and sits them in a neat row, facing away.
- She climbs up into the other armchair. Curls up.
- Closes her eyes.
- Bongo watches this with a gentle expression. His eyebrow slowly softens.
- He settles back into his chair.
- Both are asleep. "ZZZ"s float up from both of them, intertwining.
- **[VISUAL LESSON: Lulu tried to be quiet so Bongo could rest — and ended up resting too.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (10 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Lullaby version of jingle. Harp glissando ending.]**

- Freeze frame: Both asleep in matching armchairs. The wind-up toys sit in a row watching over them.
- Text: **"THE END 😴"** (in whisper-quiet font styling)

# EPISODE 4: "LULU'S PAINTING PARTY" 🎨
**Runtime: ~4.5 minutes | Lesson: Sharing**

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Jingle with a paintbrush-swoosh sound added]**

- Title card: **"Episode 4: Lulu's Painting Party 🎨"**
- A cartoon paintbrush draws the episode title text live on screen.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (45 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Artistic, flowing melody. Strings and woodwinds. Impressionist feel.]**

**SCENE: Bongo's porch. An easel is set up. A small table holds paint jars — perfectly organized by color. Clean brushes. A blank white canvas.**

- Bongo stands before the easel, stroking his chin thoughtfully.
- He dips a brush. Carefully draws one precise line.
- Steps back. Nods. Dips again. Adds a curve.
- He's painting a **still life of apples in a bowl** — very classical, very deliberate.
- He checks his reference (a real bowl of apples on a nearby table). Checks the painting. Perfect.

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: PAINT-SPLAT sound effect into Lulu's theme]**

- Lulu runs up and STOPS when she sees the paints.
- Her eyes rotate through every color — she tracks from jar to jar.
- Her hands reach out slowly, fingers wiggling.
- Bongo sees her. Steps between her and the paints. Shakes his head gently.
- He holds up one finger: *One brush. For me.*
- Lulu pouts. The most theatrical pout in animation history. Her lower lip quivers.
- Bongo softens. Picks up a TINY brush. Hands it to her with the smallest paint jar.
- Her pout vanishes instantly. Huge grin.

---

## SEGMENT 4 — ESCALATION (110 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Builds from classical painting music into increasingly wild jazz]**

### Round 1 — THE PALETTE PROBLEM
- Lulu has her tiny brush and tiny paint jar (yellow).
- She paints a sun on her section of the canvas (they each have a side now).
- She runs out of yellow. Looks at Bongo's paints.
- She tiptoes over. Takes the yellow jar. Bongo doesn't notice.
- She uses it. Runs out. Takes the orange. Then the red.
- Bongo reaches for a color. It's gone. He looks at his table. Half the jars missing.
- He follows the trail of tiny colored footprints. They lead to Lulu.
- Lulu is now surrounded by 9 open jars. Her "sun" has grown into a massive spiral covering her whole canvas side and starting onto Bongo's.
- Bongo's eye twitches.

### Round 2 — THE FINGER PAINTING INCIDENT
- Bongo reclaims his jars. Re-organizes them. Guards them.
- Lulu runs out of yellow again (she really loves yellow).
- She tries to reach past Bongo. He moves the jars.
- She tries the other side. He moves them again.
- She looks at her hands. Looks at the yellow jar. Looks at her hands.
- She dips her entire hand in the yellow jar.
- **[SFX: SPLOPP]**
- She slaps her hand on the canvas. HANDPRINT.
- She gasps in delight. Does it again. And again.
- Bongo turns around. There are yellow handprints everywhere — on the canvas, up the easel, on the porch railing, going up the wall, and inexplicably, one on Bongo's own back.
- He reaches back and touches it. Looks at his paw. Yellow.

### Round 3 — THE SNEEZE
- Bongo has almost salvaged his still-life painting. It's looking good.
- Lulu is happy, covered in paint, making something abstract.
- A butterfly lands on Lulu's nose.
- Her eyes cross looking at it.
- Her nose twitches.
- **[SFX: sniff... sniff... sniff...]**
- Bongo hears this. Slowly turns. Eyes widen.
- **[SFX: AHHHH... AHHHH... AHHHHHHCHOOOOOOOO]**
- **[SFX: SPLAT SPLAT SPLAT SPLAT SPLAT]**
- An arc of rainbow paint (she was holding multiple brushes) sprays across everything.
- Bongo's still life: gone. Now it's a rainbow abstract.
- Bongo stands. Covered in rainbow. His painting covered in rainbow. Everything: rainbow.

---

## SEGMENT 5 — CLIMAX (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Dramatic reveal music — ascending horns — then stops]**

- Bongo slowly turns the canvas around to look at it.
- His original still life is completely covered in Lulu's chaos.
- But.
- From the right angle — the accidental handprints, the sneeze rainbow, the spirals —
- It looks like the most beautiful, colorful, chaotic artwork ever.
- Bongo stares at it.
- He tilts his head one way. Then the other.
- His brow furrows. Then... unfurrows.
- **[MUSIC: One single "hmmmm" musical tone]**

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Warm, triumphant, gentle. Like a gallery opening.]**

- Lulu watches Bongo anxiously. Chews her lip.
- Bongo takes the painting off the easel. Holds it at arm's length.
- He walks it inside. Comes back with a FRAME.
- He frames the chaotic painting.
- He hangs it on the porch wall.
- Lulu's mouth drops open.
- Bongo steps back. Crosses his arms. Satisfied nod.
- Lulu grabs Bongo's paw and jumps up and down.
- Bongo smiles. They both look at the painting.
- **[VISUAL LESSON: Bongo shared his canvas — and together they made something better than either could alone.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (10 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Jingle with paint-splash SFX accents]**

- Freeze frame: Both pointing proudly at the hanging painting. Both still covered in rainbow paint.
- Text: **"THE END 🎨"**

# EPISODE 5: "THE BIRTHDAY CAKE CAPER" 🎂
**Runtime: ~4.5 minutes | Lesson: Celebrating Together**

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Birthday-party jingle version — balloons pop in background]**

- Title card: **"Episode 5: The Birthday Cake Caper 🎂"**
- Confetti rains down behind the title text.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (45 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Proud, march-like baking music. Staccato piano.]**

**SCENE: Bongo's kitchen, decorated with a single "HAPPY BIRTHDAY LULU" banner (Bongo put it up himself — slightly crooked). Ingredients on counter: flour, eggs, butter, sugar, chocolate.**

- It's Lulu's birthday. Bongo is baking her a cake — secretly (or he thinks).
- He measures flour with a tiny spoon, very precisely.
- Cracks eggs one by one. Admires each one before it goes in.
- He stirs the batter. Pours it into a round pan.
- Opens the oven. Slides it in. Sets a timer. Dusts his paws.
- He picks up a **"HAPPY BIRTHDAY LULU"** sign he made, decorated with little paw-drawn hearts.
- He looks at it warmly. He's very proud.

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (25 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Birthday fanfare — Lulu's theme with added trumpet flourish]**

- Lulu arrives wearing a **paper crown** (she made it herself, slightly lopsided).
- She pushes the door open — sees the banner — her eyes go supernova.
- She covers her mouth with both hands. Looks at Bongo.
- Bongo spreads his arms: *Surprise!*
- Lulu LAUNCHES herself at him for a hug.
- **[SFX: THWUMP — bear staggers backward]**
- She sniffs the air. The CAKE SMELL. Her eyes go wide again.
- She looks at the oven. Then at Bongo. Then at the oven.
- Bongo shakes his head: *Not yet.*
- Lulu looks at the timer. It has 15 minutes left. She stares at it. Stares at it.
- (Comedic beat: she stares intensely as if she can make it go faster.)

---

## SEGMENT 4 — ESCALATION (110 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Ticking-clock motif builds under the comedy]**

### Round 1 — THE DECORATING DISASTER
- Bongo hands Lulu frosting bags to decorate the cooling rack while he preps.
- Lulu squeezes a bag. Frosting goes everywhere except where intended.
- She tries again. It spirals up her arm.
- She accidentally squeezes the bag TOWARD Bongo.
- **[SFX: SPLAT]**
- A frosting blob hits Bongo directly in the face.
- He stands there, blue frosting on his nose.
- Lulu covers her mouth. Laughter she's trying to hold in.
- Bongo removes frosting from nose. Looks at it. Tastes it.
- Gives Lulu a look. She explodes laughing (silent, huge open mouth).

### Round 2 — THE SPRINKLE TSUNAMI
- Bongo takes over frosting. Lulu is given sprinkles as her job.
- A HUGE jar of sprinkles.
- She opens it. The lid pops off.
- **[SFX: RATTLE RATTLE RATTLE — then SHHHHHHHHH]**
- Sprinkles everywhere. The entire kitchen floor is now sprinkles.
- Bongo takes one step. Slips.
- **[SFX: CARTOON SLIP SOUND + BOINK]**
- He recovers. Takes another careful step. Slips again.
- The kitchen is an ice rink of sprinkles. Both of them slip-slide around trying to work.
- The cake on the counter slides off on its own, carried by a river of sprinkles — right to the edge.
- Bongo and Lulu both lunge for it—

### Round 3 — THE CANDLE CATASTROPHE
- The cake is saved! On the table. Bongo puts in candles — five of them.
- Lulu's eyes: pure magic.
- Bongo goes to light them. One match. Blows it out. Two. Blows it out. Three — a breeze from the window blows it out.
- He looks at the candles. Looks at Lulu. Lulu is bouncing in place, barely containing herself.
- He finally lights all five.
- Lulu's eyes reflect all five candle flames.
- He begins the slow approach to sing... but Lulu ALREADY blows out the candles.
- **[SFX: FWOOOO — then silence]**
- She looks so proud of herself.
- Bongo holds the still-unlit match. Stares at her.

---

## SEGMENT 5 — CLIMAX (30 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Grand, sweeping, ridiculous birthday fanfare]**

- Bongo re-lights the candles. Steps back. Opens his mouth to sing.
- Lulu opens hers too — clearly ready to blow them out the second he's done.
- The race is on. He speeds up his silent singing (exaggerated mouth movements, clearly rushing the Happy Birthday song).
- She leans forward more and more with each second.
- He finishes! Points at her — *NOW!*
- She blows with MAXIMUM force.
- **[SFX: WHOOOOOOOOOOSH]**
- All the candles go out.
- Also the frosting.
- The frosting is just... gone. Blown clean off the cake.
- Bongo looks at the bare naked cake.
- Then at Lulu.
- Then at the frosting, which landed on the ceiling.
- He points slowly upward.
- Lulu looks up.
- A frosting stalactite drips onto both of them.

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (40 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Sweet, celebratory waltz. Warm and glowing.]**

- Lulu picks up a spatula. Begins trying to scrape frosting off the ceiling back onto the cake.
- It's hilariously ineffective but she tries SO HARD.
- Bongo watches. Then picks up his own spatula. Helps.
- Together they manage to get SOME frosting back on the cake. It looks messy and imperfect.
- Bongo takes sprinkles (a small jar this time). Sprinkles them carefully.
- Lulu places the five candles back (two are crooked).
- Bongo re-lights them.
- This time, he points at them, then at her, then holds up his paw — *Wait.*
- He begins the birthday song (exaggerated silent singing, conducting himself).
- Lulu clasps her hands, waiting. She's barely holding it together.
- He finishes. Points to her. She takes the MOST controlled, gentle blow —
- **[SFX: soft fwoo]**
- All five out. Perfect.
- Both throw their arms up. **[SFX: PARTY HORN TOOT × 3]**
- They cut the messy, imperfect cake. Bongo holds out the first slice to Lulu.
- **[VISUAL LESSON: A celebration is better when you share it — even a messy one.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (10 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Jingle with party horn accents and confetti SFX]**

- Freeze frame: Lulu with a huge slice of cake. Bongo wearing a party hat. Frosting still on ceiling above them.
- Text: **"THE END 🎂 HAPPY BIRTHDAY LULU!"**

# EPISODE 6 (BONUS): "GOODNIGHT, FOREST" 🌙
**Runtime: ~3 minutes | Lesson: Taking Care of Each Other**

*(Intended as the final episode — acts as a calming wind-down for young viewers)*

---

## SEGMENT 1 — INTRO (15 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Slowest, most gentle jingle version. Almost a lullaby.]**

- Title card: **"Episode 6: Goodnight, Forest 🌙"**
- Stars twinkle behind the text.

---

## SEGMENT 2 — SETUP (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Quiet evening forest sounds. Crickets. Gentle flute.]**

**SCENE: Outside Bongo's cabin. Evening. Deep blue-purple sky with cartoon stars. Fireflies blinking. The forest is peaceful and glowing.**

- Bongo sits on his porch steps, looking at the stars.
- He holds a mug of something warm (steam rises in gentle spirals).
- He traces constellations with one finger. Happy. Peaceful.
- The fireflies blink around him. An owl hoots once.

---

## SEGMENT 3 — LULU ARRIVES (20 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Gentle, no tempo spike this time — soft arrival]**

- Lulu appears from the treeline carrying a **jar for catching fireflies**.
- She spots Bongo. Sits next to him without crashing into anything.
- (The audience notices this. Bongo notices this. He looks at her sideways.)
- She holds up her empty jar. Points to a firefly.
- Then looks at Bongo. Waits for permission.
- Bongo smiles. Nods.

---

## SEGMENT 4 — GENTLE SEQUENCE (80 seconds)

*[This episode replaces the standard escalation with a calm, wonder-filled sequence]*

**[MUSIC: Soft, wonder-filled. Delicate piano and strings. Slow tempo.]**

### Moment 1 — THE FIREFLY CHASE
- Lulu tiptoes after a firefly. Reaches out slowly. It blinks away.
- She tries again. It blinks away. 
- She looks at Bongo. He watches. Points upward — there are hundreds.
- She looks up. Eyes go wide with wonder (not chaos-excitement — pure awe).
- She opens the jar. Waits. A firefly drifts into it.
- She holds it up to her face, watching it glow.

### Moment 2 — SHARING THE JAR
- She walks to Bongo. Holds the jar between them.
- They both watch the firefly glow.
- She looks at Bongo. He looks at her.
- She opens the jar. The firefly flies out.
- She watches it go, then smiles. Bongo smiles too.

### Moment 3 — STARGAZING
- Bongo points at a constellation. Moves his finger to trace the shape.
- Lulu follows his finger, eyes wide.
- She points at another cluster. Traces a shape — it's clearly a cartoon bear.
- Bongo looks at her traced constellation. It's him. He laughs (big silent bear laugh, belly shaking).
- Lulu grins and bounces once, then settles.

---

## SEGMENT 5 — NO CLIMAX (This episode ends gently)

**[MUSIC: Lullaby transition. Slower and slower.]**

- The stars get brighter. The fireflies circle the porch.
- Lulu leans against Bongo's arm. Her eyes get heavy.
- Bongo notices. He doesn't move. Just smiles down at her.
- Her eyes close. She's asleep.
- Bongo carefully lifts her — gently, like carrying something precious.
- He carries her inside.

---

## SEGMENT 6 — RESOLUTION (35 seconds)

**[MUSIC: Pure lullaby now. Harp and soft celesta.]**

- Bongo tucks Lulu into a small cozy bed (his guest bed — patchwork quilt, stuffed animals).
- He places her headband on the nightstand. Tucks in her red shoes beside it.
- He dims a small lantern. Leaves the door slightly open.
- He goes back to his porch steps.
- He looks up at the stars.
- He finds Lulu's constellation — the bear she traced.
- He smiles. Sips his mug.
- **[VISUAL LESSON: Taking care of someone you love is the best thing you can do.]**

---

## SEGMENT 7 — OUTRO (15 seconds — extended for compilation close)

**[MUSIC: Full lullaby version of the Lulu & Bongo theme. Slow and warm.]**

- Freeze frame: Bongo on the porch under the stars. Inside, through the window, Lulu sleeping. Fireflies circling.
- Text appears slowly: **"THE END 🌙"**
- Then: **"Goodnight from Lulu & Bongo!"**
- Final logo card: **"LULU & BONGO"** — same as opening, but with moon and stars.
- Gentle fade to black.
- **[MUSIC: Last note holds, then soft fade out]**
